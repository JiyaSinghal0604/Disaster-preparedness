Wildfire Safety & Preparedness

One spark can destroy—awareness and action can save.

Causes of Wildfires

  • Dry weather and high winds
  • Human activities (campfires, discarded cigarettes)
  • Lightning strikes

Warning Signs

  • Smoke and smell of burning
  • Advancing flames in vegetation
  • Official fire alerts

Preparation Tips

  • Clear dry leaves and flammable material around homes
  • Keep firefighting tools accessible
  • Prepare evacuation routes and kits

During a Wildfire

  • Follow evacuation orders immediately
  • Keep windows and doors closed
  • Use masks to reduce smoke inhalation
