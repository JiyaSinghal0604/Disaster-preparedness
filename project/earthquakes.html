Earthquake Safety & Preparedness

The ground may shake, but preparedness keeps you steady.

Causes of Earthquakes

  • Movement of tectonic plates
  • Volcanic activity and magma pressure
  • Man-made underground explosions

Warning Signs

  • Sudden ground shaking
  • Unusual animal behavior
  • Changes in water levels or ground cracks

Preparation Tips

  • Secure heavy furniture and appliances
  • Prepare an emergency survival kit
  • Identify safe spots in every room
  • Practice “Drop, Cover, and Hold On”

During an Earthquake

  • Drop to the ground immediately
  • Take cover under a strong table or bed
  • Stay away from windows and glass
  • Avoid running outside during shaking
