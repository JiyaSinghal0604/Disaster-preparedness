Drought Preparedness

“Drought does not begin with an empty river — it begins with wasted water.”

Causes of Droughts

  • Low rainfall over long periods
  • Extreme heat and climate change
  • Overuse of groundwater and rivers

Warning Signs

  • Decline in river and groundwater levels
  • Crop failure and dry soil
  • Long dry spells, cracked land

Preparation Tips

  • Install rainwater harvesting systems
  • Grow drought-resistant crops
  • Store emergency drinking water
  • Use efficient irrigation like drip systems

During a Drought

  • Reduce non-essential water usage
  • Follow water rationing guidelines
  • Reuse household water when possible
  • Support community water-saving programs

Do's and Don'ts

✔ Do's

  • Take short baths instead of long showers
  • Fix leaking taps immediately
  • Store and use water wisely
  • Reuse RO and AC water for plants

✘ Don'ts

  • Don't leave taps running unnecessarily
  • Don't wash vehicles frequently
  • Don't waste garden water
  • Don't ignore early signs of drought
