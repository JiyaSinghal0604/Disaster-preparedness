Landslide Awareness & Safety

When the earth moves, quick decisions save lives.

Causes of Landslides

  • Heavy rainfall on steep slopes
  • Deforestation and unstable terrain
  • Earthquakes and soil erosion

Warning Signs

  • New cracks on the ground or road
  • Trees or poles tilting unusually
  • Unusual rumbling or soil movement sounds

Preparation Tips

  • Avoid construction on steep slopes
  • Plant vegetation to stabilize soil
  • Maintain proper drainage systems

During a Landslide

  • Move away from the slope immediately
  • Do not walk on or cross fresh debris
  • Follow official emergency instructions
