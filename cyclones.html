Cyclone Safety & Preparedness

Strong winds test us, strong preparation saves us.

Causes of Cyclones

  • Warm ocean water
  • High humidity leading to rapid evaporation
  • Formation of low-pressure systems

Warning Signs

  • Powerful winds increasing gradually
  • Heavy rainfall and rough sea conditions
  • Weather alerts and advisories

Preparation Tips

  • Reinforce windows, doors, and rooftops
  • Store extra food, water, and first-aid supplies
  • Charge mobile phones and power banks
  • Locate the nearest cyclone shelter

During a Cyclone

  • Stay indoors and keep away from windows
  • Turn off electricity to avoid hazards
  • Evacuate immediately if instructed
