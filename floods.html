Flood Safety & Preparedness

When waters rises, preparedness keeps you afloat.
Flooding caused by continuous rainfall leading to rising water levels.

Causes of Floods

  • Heavy rainfall
  • Overflowing rivers and dams
  • Poor drainage systems
  • Coastal storm surges

Warning Signs

  • Continuous heavy rainfall
  • Rapid rise in water levels
  • Weather alerts and warnings

Preparation Tips

  • Store drinking water and food
  • Keep an emergency kit ready
  • Charge all electronic devices
  • Know your area's evacuation routes

During a Flood

  • Avoid walking or driving through flood water
  • Move to higher and safer ground
  • Turn off electricity
